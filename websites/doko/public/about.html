Our Story

Welcome to Doko Nepal, your number one source for all things fresh and essential. We're dedicated to giving you the very best of groceries, with a focus on quality, customer service, and uniqueness.

Founded in 2025, Doko Nepal has come a long way from its beginnings. When we first started out, our passion for providing fresh, locally-sourced produce drove us to do intense research, and gave us the impetus to turn hard work and inspiration into a booming online store. We now serve customers all over the nation and are thrilled to be a part of the fair-trade wing of the grocery industry.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide our customers with the freshest products at the best prices, delivered right to their doorstep. We believe in sustainable farming and supporting local communities. We hope you enjoy our products as much as we enjoy offering them to you. If you have any questions or comments, please don't hesitate to contact us.
